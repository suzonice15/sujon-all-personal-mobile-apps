## 1. Summary

- You can read all lessons and take quizzes **without an account**.
- An account is **optional**. If you create one, we collect your name, email, phone number and the profile details you enter.
- Earning and withdrawal features use a **device identifier** so that coins can be tracked without login.
- Bookmarks, settings, quiz results and the coin ledger are stored **on your device**; coin, referral and withdrawal records are also synced to our server.
- The app shows ads through **Google AdMob** and its mediation partners. They may collect device identifiers and approximate location as described below.
- You can request deletion of your account and server data by emailing **suzonice15@gmail.com**.

---

## 2. Information you provide

### 2.1 Account (optional)

When you register, we collect:

- name
- email address
- phone number
- password (used only for login)
- gender
- district
- address (optional)
- referral code, if you entered one

You can use the reading and quiz features without creating an account. Login is needed only for the profile, referral and cross-device account features.

### 2.2 Withdrawal (bKash)

When you request a withdrawal we collect:

- your bKash account number
- the amount and the number of coins used
- the fee, status and any transaction reference

This information is used only to review and pay your withdrawal, to prevent fraud, and to keep the records required for accounting.

### 2.3 Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, including any screenshot or device details you attach.

---

## 3. Information collected automatically

### 3.1 Device identifier

The app reads your device's unique identifier (Android ID) through the device-info library and sends it to our server with coin, referral and withdrawal requests. We use it to keep your coin balance consistent and to prevent duplicate reward claims. It is not used for advertising by us.

### 3.2 Usage records

We store records of coins earned and spent, quiz activity needed for rewards, referral clicks and withdrawals. These records are linked to your device identifier and, if you registered, to your account.

### 3.3 Technical data

Our server receives your IP address, request time and basic app version information as part of normal HTTPS requests and server logs.

### 3.4 Advertising data

See section 5.

---

## 4. Information stored only on your device

The app keeps a private database on your phone containing:

- bookmarks
- reading progress and quiz results
- coin ledger and notification history
- withdrawal history
- app settings (theme, sound, notification preference)

This local data is not uploaded except for the coin, referral and withdrawal records described above. You can delete it with Android Settings → Apps → নারী ও ইসলাম → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 5. Advertising and third parties

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) with mediation adapters for **Meta Audience Network**, **Unity Ads** and **AppLovin**. These SDKs may collect and process:

- **Device or other identifiers**, including the advertising ID and app set ID
- **Approximate location**, derived from the IP address (the app does not request GPS)
- **Device information**, such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
- **Ad interactions**, such as impressions, clicks and ad performance

This data is used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve the partners' services. Some of it may be shared with the partners listed above.

We do not receive your name, email, precise location or contacts from the ads SDK. We see only aggregate reports such as impressions, clicks and estimated revenue.

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data in apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Meta: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy
- Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
- AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy
- Opt out of personalized ads: https://adssettings.google.com

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

---

## 6. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to:

- provide the reading, quiz, coin, referral and withdrawal features (contract)
- keep your account and coin balance secure and prevent fraud (legitimate interest)
- display and measure advertisements (consent where required, otherwise legitimate interest)
- respond to support requests (contract or legitimate interest)
- comply with accounting and legal obligations

Where local law requires consent for personalized advertising, you can withdraw it through your device ad settings or by contacting us. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland and no consent dialog is shown, the app serves non-personalized ads only.

---

## 7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with:

- **Our backend host**, which stores your account, coin, referral and withdrawal records on our behalf
- **Google AdMob and the mediation partners named in section 5**, for advertising and fraud prevention
- **bKash or the payment channel**, to pay an approved withdrawal
- authorities, when required by law

We do not share your bookmarks or reading progress, because those stay on your device.

---

## 8. Retention

- Account data: kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
- Coin, referral and withdrawal records: kept up to 24 months after the last activity, or longer where accounting law requires.
- Support emails: kept up to 24 months.
- Local data: kept until you clear the app data or uninstall.
- Advertising data: retained by Google and the partners under their own policies.

---

## 9. Security

Account and reward requests use HTTPS. Local data is protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the data we collect to what the features need.

---

## 10. Children

The app is intended for users aged **18 and older** because it includes a cash-reward and withdrawal feature. It is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability.

- **Local data:** delete it in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
- **Account and server data:** email **suzonice15@gmail.com** from your registered email address (or state your registered phone number) and ask for deletion. We delete the account and associated coin, referral and withdrawal records within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
- **Advertising data:** use Google Ad Settings, or contact us and we will forward your request to Google where applicable.

We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

---

## 12. International transfers

Our server and Google may process data in countries outside your own. They rely on legal mechanisms such as standard contractual clauses. See the partners' privacy policies for details.

## 13. Third-party links

The app may open external links (for example the Play Store page, the developer page or a partner site). Those sites have their own privacy policies.
