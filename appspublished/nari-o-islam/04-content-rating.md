# 04 — Content Rating (IARC questionnaire)

Play Console → **App content → Content rating**. Choose **Education** (Reference is also acceptable), then answer:

## Category

```
Education
```

## Questions and answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Violence (realistic or fantasy) | No |
| Blood or gore | No |
| Sexual content or nudity | No |
| Profanity or crude humour | No |
| Alcohol, tobacco or drugs | No |

| Horror or fear elements | No |
| Social features (chat, forums, user profiles) | No |

## Expected result

- **IARC:** Everyone / 3+
- **PEGI:** 3
- **ESRB:** Everyone
- **USK:** 0

The ads declaration is the only item that can affect the result; ads alone normally still produce "Everyone".

## Notes for this app

1. **Sensitive topics are handled educationally.** The app discusses women's rights, hijab, marriage, menstruation (হায়েজ/নিফাস) and family life in an Islamic educational context. There is no sexual content, no nudity and no explicit imagery.
2. **Reward box is not gambling.** The daily box and claim rewards are free, need no purchase, and cannot be wagered. Coins convert to bKash only through the withdrawal flow, as a loyalty reward.
3. **No social features.** The referral screen shows totals from our own server; there is no chat, comment, forum or in-app posting.
4. **No political or sectarian campaigning.** The content aims to explain widely accepted Islamic teachings. Where schools of thought differ, the app advises consulting a qualified scholar.
5. **Target audience is 18+** because of the bKash withdrawal feature, even though the content itself is suitable for all ages. Declared separately in `05-app-content.md`.
6. If chat, comments or public profiles are added later, redo this questionnaire and add moderation.
