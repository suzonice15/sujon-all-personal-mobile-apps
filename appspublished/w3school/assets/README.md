## Mark

`</>` drawn with rounded white strokes and a soft baseline, on a teal gradient. No text inside the icon, so it stays readable at 48 px.

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of one stroke.
- Do not stretch, recolour or add text to the icon.
- Do not place the icon on a busy photo without a solid backing.
- For the Play icon use the 512 full-bleed file; Google applies the rounded mask.
