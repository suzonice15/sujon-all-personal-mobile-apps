## 1. Summary

- We do **not** require an account and we do **not** collect your name, email or phone number.
- Your learning progress, quiz results, bookmarks and settings are stored **only on your device**.
- The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Google may collect device and advertising data as described below.
- You can delete all local data by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.

---

## 2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally in a private database on your phone:

- lesson and chapter progress
- quiz scores and answers
- bookmarks and interview questions marked as practiced
- app settings (theme, quiz sound, notification preference)

This information is **not transmitted to us**. It leaves the device only if you back up your phone with a service you control, or if you share a screenshot.

Deleting the app, or using Android Settings → Apps → CodeNest → Storage → Clear data, removes it.

---

## 3. Information collected by advertising partners

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, rewarded and native ads. According to Google, the SDK may collect and process:

- **Device or other identifiers**, including the advertising ID and app set ID
- **Approximate location**, derived from the IP address (not GPS)
- **Device information**, such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
- **Ad interactions**, such as impressions, clicks and ad performance

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve its services. Some of this data may be shared with Google's advertising partners.

We do not receive your name, email, precise location or contacts from AdMob. We receive only aggregate reports such as impressions, clicks, and estimated revenue.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data in partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Opt out of personalized ads: https://adssettings.google.com

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

---

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to:

- display and measure advertisements (legitimate interest; consent where required by law)
- keep the app stable and prevent ad fraud (legitimate interest)
- respond to support requests you send us (contract or legitimate interest)

Where local law requires consent for personalized advertising, you can withdraw it through your device ad settings or by contacting us. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland and no consent dialog is shown, the app serves non-personalized ads only.

---

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with:

- **Google AdMob / Google LLC**, for advertising and fraud prevention
- authorities, when required by law

We do not share your learning progress because we never receive it.

---

## 6. Retention

- Local learning data: kept until you clear the app data or uninstall.
- Advertising data: retained by Google according to its own policies.
- Support emails: kept up to 24 months, then deleted.

---

## 7. Security

Local data is protected by Android's app sandbox. Network requests made by the ads SDK use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the app free of accounts and sensitive data to reduce risk.

---

## 8. Children

The app is intended for users aged **13 and older**. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability.

- Local learning data: delete it in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
- Advertising data: use Google Ad Settings, or contact us and we will forward your request to Google where applicable.
- Support emails: email **[EMAIL]** and we will delete them.

We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 10. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries outside your own. Google relies on legal mechanisms such as standard contractual clauses. See Google's privacy policy for details.
