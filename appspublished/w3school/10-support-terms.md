## FAQ (add to the website or a Help screen)

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes. Lessons, quizzes and interview questions are stored on the device. Ads may need a connection.

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no login and no cloud sync.

**Where is my progress stored?**
In a local database on your phone. Uninstalling or clearing app data deletes it.

**Why do I see ads?**
Ads keep the app free. They are provided by Google AdMob.

**Can I remove ads?**
Not in version 1.0. A one-time purchase may be added later.

**A lesson looks wrong.**
Email the course, chapter and the correction. Content is reviewed and fixed in updates.

**Is the content a certification?**
No. CodeNest is a study aid, not an accredited course or certificate.

**Why does the app request storage permission?**
It should not. Version 1.0 removes unused storage permissions; if your build still asks, update to the latest version.
